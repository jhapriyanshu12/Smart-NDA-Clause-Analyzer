SAMPLE NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
For classroom software testing only — fictional text.

1. Purpose
The parties may exchange confidential information solely to evaluate a possible business relationship.

2. Confidential Information
Confidential information includes non-public business, technical, and financial information disclosed in written or oral form and identified as confidential where reasonably practicable.

3. Permitted Use
The receiving party may use confidential information only for the purpose stated in this agreement.

4. Exclusions
Confidential information does not include information that becomes public without breach, was lawfully known before disclosure, is independently developed without use of confidential information, or is lawfully received from another source without a duty of confidentiality.

5. Protection
The receiving party will use reasonable care to protect confidential information and will limit access to people who need it for the stated purpose and are subject to appropriate confidentiality duties.

6. Duration
This agreement remains in effect for two years. Confidentiality duties for information disclosed during the term continue for three years after the agreement ends.

7. Return or Deletion
Upon written request, the receiving party will return or delete confidential information, except for copies retained in routine backups or where retention is required by law.

8. Legal Process
If legally compelled to disclose confidential information, the receiving party may disclose the legally required portion and, where permitted, provide advance notice.

9. Governing Law
The agreement is governed by the laws of the fictional State of Example.

10. General
Any amendment must be in writing and accepted by both parties.
